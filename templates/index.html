Frequently asked questions

Plan details and how the studio works.

Do plans renew automatically?

No. Both are one-time payments. The annual plan lasts 365 days; lifetime access does not expire.

How much transcription is included?

The annual plan includes 3,600 minutes and lifetime includes 1,000,000 minutes per 30 days. Each task is limited to 60 minutes.

Is voice generation included in the paid plan?

Edge TTS voice generation remains a free trial. Both paid plans unlock audio transcription with faster-whisper.

Can I buy the plan in this local preview?

No. This local preview does not connect to sign-in or checkout. Online purchases become available after the service is configured.
